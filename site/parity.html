How to read this. A competitor row marked paid tier means the tool does have the capability, but behind an extension, add-on or metered service — a different answer to “can I do this today” than “included”, so it is counted separately rather than folded into either. Kerf has no tiers, so its rows are only yes, partial or no.

What this page is not. It is not a verdict, and the totals are not scores. The rows were written from Kerf's feature set, which is why Kerf is a yes on 98.9% of them — that number says something about how the list was built, not about the products. A tool scoring low here may be far better than Kerf at the work you actually do. If you are choosing between them, read the rows in your discipline and ignore the counts.
